# SHAPE-010 — A causal counterexample becomes a new observed basis direction

**Scope:** A native, complete, source-witnessed fourth Qwen layer-0 output (the actual layer-1 input) can extend SHAPE-009's source-qualified two-variable normalization Ground into a separate, monotone three-variable Ground. This is about an **observer-specific normalization operation**, not a proved new next-token transition.

## Architecture and invariant

SHAPE-009 observes consecutive actual native transitions 0, 1, 2 and admits only the triangle between their layer-0 outputs. It separately inspects the actual fourth native transition 3 and rejects it when the orthogonal residual to that plane is nonzero. SHAPE-010 calls the same qualified-capture validator as its parent to recheck the fourth original semantic causal-chain head, F64 interlayer handoff digest, position, previous head and trained-layer receipt. It rejects a caller-supplied vector without this native witness.

The child first **reconstructs SHAPE-009 from its externally pinned checkpoint** and validates its original three E-CPU CAS vectors and normalization gains. It never rewrites the parent. The fourth actual hidden vector becomes a new E-CPU ComputationalObjectStore object with its own page backing, SHA-256, original native F64 transfer digest and chain record.

SHAPE-010 records the original rank-2 orthogonal projection residual for the fourth real causal state. It requires *affine rank expansion*: a position that is outside the original triangle but **still inside its affine plane** is not sufficient. The new source digest binds the pinned parent Ground, parent checkpoint and old native causal head to the fourth native receipt, new E-CPU manifest, vector SHA-256 and monotonically increasing source revision **4**. Previously generated SHAPE-009 responses remain valid only under the unchanged original SHAPE-009 Ground.

## Learned, independently checked mathematical relation

For actual source states \(h_0,h_1,h_2,h_3\), the child Ground admits the mathematical simplex

\[
x(s,t,u)=h_0+s(h_1-h_0)+t(h_2-h_0)+u(h_3-h_0),\quad
s,t,u\ge 0,\quad s+t+u\le1.
\]

As in SHAPE-009, these are hypothetical mathematical vectors formed from genuine contextual source states. **Only the four vertices have actually been executed as original causal states.** A simplex interpolation is not proof of a fifth real recurrent transition.

Ten native normalization observations, at the origin, three unit vertices, three half-vertices and three pair midpoints, determine the ten coefficients of an unknown general quadratic energy in \(s,t,u\). The fit is derived from those observed original native outputs, rather than supplied by the Gram identity. Four independently observed held-out native outputs then challenge constant, affine, diagonal-quadratic and complete-quadratic hypotheses. The three weaker hypotheses must fail the stated output tolerance, and the complete law must pass. A hypothesis whose inferred energy becomes nonpositive at a challenge is rejected as *inadmissible*, not reported as a measured output error: its proof contains an explicit invalid-energy candidate list and a finite JSON-safe rejection sentinel.

After fitting, an independent, source-bound Gram calculation of the four actual hidden vectors checks all ten inferred coefficients. This is a certificate of the **normalization-on-this-simplex** identity, not proof of future recurrent-state dynamics.

A sparse requested output coordinate \(i\) is rendered from the learned scalar energy and exactly five E-CPU coordinate pages (the four actual hidden states plus original trained gain):

\[
y_i = x_i(s,t,u)(1+w_i)/\sqrt{E(s,t,u)+10^{-6}}.
\]

A newly requested observer coordinate is source-/revision-/causal-head-/intention-/predecessor-bound. The event explicitly records zero native trained calls during generation and a scope that is **not** a native causal continuation. Held-out results are formed before calling the separately retained VL-64 native normalization reference. Full-width generation incurs full-width costs; no blanket acceleration is claimed.

## Source-free recovery and falsification

The child checkpoint retains the complete pinned parent checkpoint, four-position source lineage, fourth native witness, E-CPU page/object backing, learned coefficients, ten training energies, four challenges, rejected-hypothesis scores, generated-event hashes, and source/ground/observer heads.

Reconstruction independently revalidates the parent, fourth E-CPU bytes and actual causal-chain receipt, rank residual, Gram-based coefficients and probe energies, competing candidate errors, every previous observer output and its exact position. It refuses stale heads, altered pages, forged native transfer receipts, altered coefficients, rehashed fake outputs and invalid/out-of-simplex intentions. No network access or complete trained GDN layer execution is needed to restore an already pinned Ground.
